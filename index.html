How to Play

Controls

WASD / arrows — walk
E / Space — act, talk, harvest
1–6 — pick a tool
R — cycle seeds
B — backpack
Esc — pause

On touch screens use the joystick and the A button. Tap the hotbar to switch tools.

Your first days

1. Take the hoe and till the soil south of the field sign.
2. Buy seeds at the supermarket in town, select the seed pouch and plant.
3. Water crops each day, then sleep at the farmhouse door.
4. Harvest, then sell at the shipping bin beside the field.
5. Fish at the river or beach, forage in the forest, and befriend the town.

Money & energy

Every action costs a little energy. Sleep at home to restore it. The clinic can heal you, the restaurant serves energy-rich meals, and the bar is a cozy place to unwind.

Crops only grow on days they are watered — unless it rains or you buy a sprinkler from the carpenter.
